## 1. Infra: TWO root-caused failures took the stack down tonight

- **ACE died from its own console log.** `~/ace_console.log` (the 1117
  restart's console redirect) grew to **12,463,747,072 bytes of pure
  `ACE >> ` prompt spam** and filled `/` to 100% (ACE last log write
  02:43:59Z). Mechanism: the durable FIFO `~/ace_stdin.fifo` had no
  persistent writer; when the last writer closed, `Console.ReadLine`
  hit EOF and ACE spun printing its prompt at CPU speed (~12 GB in
  ~4.5 h). **Fix (relaunch recipe change):** launch with stdin opened
  READ-WRITE on the FIFO so the process itself holds a writer and
  ReadLine blocks instead of EOF-spinning, and send console output to
  the big drive:
  `cd $ACERT && setsid nohup dotnet ACE.Server.dll 0<> ~/ace_stdin.fifo >> /mnt/wbterminal2/ace_console.log 2>&1 &`
  Verified: world open 00:48:54 NDT (pid 3524175), exactly one prompt
  in the console log, ACE_Log appending. ⚠ ace-live.md's recipe
  predates this — it needs the `0<>` correction (memory edits are
  user-directed; recorded here until then). Also freed 3.4 GB of stale
  /tmp session scratchpads; root now ~87%.

  restart, logins REACHED ACE and verified ("client tailnet1 connected
