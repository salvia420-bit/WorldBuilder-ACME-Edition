Verdicts:
1. **warmPark (`?warmPark=on`, Phase 9a) is consistently ~9–11 % faster
   full-cycle and stability-neutral.** Its win is ring-overlap/revisit
   re-attach; first-visit cold bake is unchanged (correct — nothing to park).
2. **The at-cap reclaim↔ring-loader ping-pong is the dominant settle cost**
   (~75 reclaim ops/stop measured): re-BAKE per bounce in classic mode,
   cheap re-ATTACH under warmPark. On the 1070 at quality=high the default
   path needs >25 s to stop churning after a town teleport — real
   user-facing pain, NOT a SwiftShader artifact.
3. **ringFloor=ringMax was A/B'd and is a NEGATIVE result** (churn worsened;
   suspected stale reclaim center right after teleport — the documented
   `pose.landblockId` freeze). Param kept (default 1), finding documented at
   both seams (landblock_lru.js ctor + index.js construction).
4. SwiftShader renderer dies every ~15–40 stops under teleport churn (both
   modes; laptop-only — the 1070 ran 62 stops clean). `--resume` absorbs it.
5. Settle-stability is NOT completeness: a streaming-starved page "settles"
   fast. Always read settle beside the lru curve. (First warmPark attempt
   looked 10× faster while baking nothing — crashed-chromium zombies had
   starved it.)
